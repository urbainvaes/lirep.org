Website guide

Lirep.org

Build an opening repertoire, compare it with real games, and see how well your preparation holds up.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with your Lichess account.
  2. Create a study and choose whether you are preparing for White or Black.
  3. Add moves on the board, or choose popular replies from Opening Explorer.
  4. Save the study, then calculate its statistics from the Stats page.

Studies

A study is a named repertoire tree with a main line and variations. Play moves on the board or click the move list to navigate. Opponent moves can branch; your own prepared position has one reply, keeping the repertoire unambiguous.

  • Set an optional starting point to calculate stats from a later position.
  • Use Opening Explorer to browse player or Masters games, filter player ratings and time controls, and add a move with one click.
  • Enable Stockfish for local analysis, candidate moves, and evaluations.
  • Explorer settings are saved per study. The studied side is fixed when the study is created.
Detailed Studies behavior

Stats

Calculations follow the study tree and weight opponent replies by their frequency in the selected Lichess Explorer pool. The studied player is assumed to know and play the prepared moves.

  • Expected score: expected fraction of a point from real-game win/draw/loss results.
  • Coverage: how often games remain inside the prepared tree, move by move.
  • Expected evaluation: Stockfish's expected position value when preparation ends.

Update evaluations in the browser first for faster expected-evaluation calculations. Win probability and expected evaluation can be recalculated separately; the Stats list runs both.

Other pages

Profile shows Lichess bullet, blitz, rapid, and classical ratings. The home page and other navigation items are placeholders; play, puzzles, and training modes are not currently implemented.

Data and requirements

Lichess sign-in is required for studies and Explorer data. Lirep uses Lichess account, board preference, and opening data, and runs Stockfish locally in your browser. Studies are stored in the app's SQLite database, associated with the Lichess account that created them. Explorer results are cached for up to 24 hours.

Stats are estimates, not guarantees. Explorer move lists omit rare replies, ratings use current rating bands unless pinned, and local engine analysis uses a fixed search depth.
