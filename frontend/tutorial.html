Tutorial

Home

Your studies, by side: one per opening you prepare, for White or for Black. + starts a new one; Edit opens it, Practice drills it.

  1. The study's tier, from its expected score: click it to see the stats.
  2. The expected score: wins plus half the draws, if you always play your prepared moves.

Editor

Play your moves on the board, or click them in the Opening Explorer; Stockfish is a switch away. One move of yours per position, as many replies as you like. Your opponent's replies fold away, each with its figures.

  1. The study's expected score, kept up to date as you edit.
  2. For each reply: the share of games reaching it · the expected score from there; (5): the moves folded inside.
  3. The replies your study doesn't have, together.
  4. ✓: the move is in your study.
  5. A popular reply that isn't, yet: click it to add it.
  6. Settings: which games the Explorer shows, and how the end of a line is valued.

Stats

How good the preparation is, against the moves players really choose in the Lichess Explorer. Recalculate after editing.

  1. The study's tier: click it for the thresholds.
  2. The settings: which games, and how lines' ends are valued.
  3. The expected score.
  4. Its wins, draws and losses.
  5. Coverage: the share of games still in your preparation after Black's 2nd move.

Practice

Two ways to learn your lines.

  1. Your practice over the last year.
  2. Knowledge: how well you know the study's moves now; it fades with time.
  3. Bullet score: runs that reached the end of the preparation, out of all runs.
  4. Practice: drills your moves, the ones you're about to forget first. Raises the knowledge.
  5. Bullet: your preparation against the clock. Raises the Bullet score.

Bullet

Reach the end of your preparation before the clock runs out. The computer plays your study's replies; a wrong move ends the run, and the next run retries the same line.

  1. Options: restart by itself after a success, and how the next line is chosen.
  2. Successes in the last month, out of all runs then.
  3. Start a run (or Enter): 1 minute, plus 2 seconds a move.
  4. Your clock: it runs only on your moves.
  5. The computer answers as players do in the Explorer, if this browser has the data.

Community

Studies are shared by default: compare yours, and import others'.

  1. Rank the shared studies by expected score, or by Stockfish's evaluation.
  2. The study's expected score.
  3. The games it was calculated with: compare rows with the same.
  4. Copy the study into yours.
